Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love every purchase. If something isn't right, here is how we make it right.

Return Eligibility

We accept returns within 7 days of confirmed delivery, provided:

  • The item is unopened and in original, undamaged packaging
  • The bottle seal is fully intact
  • The item was not purchased during a final-sale event
  • Proof of purchase is provided

Exchanges

Opened items may be exchanged if a verified manufacturing defect or incorrect item was received. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. Approved exchanges are processed at no additional cost.

How to Initiate a Return

Contact us with your order number and reason before shipping anything back. Unrequested returns cannot be processed.

Refund Processing

Once a return is received and inspected, refunds are issued within 5–7 business days via the original payment method.
